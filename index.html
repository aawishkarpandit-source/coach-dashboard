1. What this app is for

Coach Dashboard is made for classroom teaching: managing students and marks, reading textbooks, and presenting lessons on a second screen. Please use it for lawful educational purposes only.

2. Your data

Student names, marks, imported books, and presentations are stored on your own computer first, and mirrored to the school's private cloud (Supabase) for sync between computers. Presentations are never uploaded — they stay on each computer. The team cannot see or sell your data; keep your cloud keys private.

3. Your responsibility

You are responsible for the accuracy of marks and student records, for having the right to use any books or media you import, and for keeping the installed computers secure (lock screens, careful with the Sync keys).

4. No warranty

The app is provided as-is for schools, free of charge. We work hard to keep it reliable (offline-first, auto-saved), but we cannot guarantee it will be error-free — please keep your own backups of important records (CSV export is built in).

5. Updates

When online, the app may download updates from the project's GitHub releases. By updating you accept the newest version of these terms.

6. Contact

Questions or problems? Reach the developer through your school coordinator.
